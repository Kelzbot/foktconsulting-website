Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 June 2026

1. Who We Are

FOKT Consulting LLC ("we", "us", "our") operates the website at foktconsulting.com and the products and services offered through it, including our software application NofoDesk. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information across our services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when you create an account or subscribe, such as your name, email address, and billing details. We also process the documents and content you upload in order to provide the features of our services, such as the extraction and organisation features of NofoDesk. Uploaded documents are processed in memory only to produce your results; they are not written to long-term storage on our servers or forwarded to third parties after processing is complete. When you make a payment, your payment information is collected and processed directly by our third-party payment processors; we do not store full card numbers. We also collect basic technical data such as your IP address and usage information to operate and improve the Service.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and operate the Service, process subscriptions and payments, communicate with you, provide support, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations. We do not use the documents you upload for any purpose other than providing the Service to you.

4. Sharing of Information

We share information with payment processors to complete subscription transactions and with service providers who help us operate our services — including cloud hosting and infrastructure providers, database providers, error-monitoring and uptime-monitoring providers, and, where you enable them, third-party integrations such as Google Drive and OneDrive. We may also disclose information where required by law. We do not sell your personal information. We do not use the documents you upload to train AI models, and we do not share them except as needed to provide the service you requested.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encryption of sensitive credentials at rest, access controls that restrict each account to its own data, rate limiting, and audit logging of key account events. You can review your account's audit log within the application's privacy settings. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. To make a request, contact us at the address below.

7. International Users

FOKT Consulting LLC is based in the United States. By using the Service, you understand that your information may be processed in the United States and other countries.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent changes.

9. Contact

Privacy questions: support@foktconsulting.com
