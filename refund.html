Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 5 June 2026

1. Overview

This policy explains how cancellations and refunds are handled for the subscription products and services operated by FOKT Consulting LLC, including our software application NofoDesk. Where a product is sold as a monthly or annual subscription, this policy applies to those subscriptions.

2. Free Trial

Where a free trial is offered, you will not be charged during the trial period. If you do not cancel before the trial ends, your subscription will begin and your payment method will be charged the applicable fee. You can cancel at any time during the trial from your account settings to avoid being charged.

3. Cancelling Your Subscription

You may cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings or by contacting us at support@foktconsulting.com. When you cancel, your subscription remains active until the end of the current billing period, and you will not be charged again. We do not provide prorated refunds for the unused portion of a billing period except as described below or where required by law.

4. Refunds

Subscription fees are generally non-refundable once a billing period has begun. However, if you are charged in error, if you cancel within a short time of an unintended renewal, or if you experience a significant problem with the Service that we are unable to resolve, you may request a refund by contacting us at support@foktconsulting.com. We review refund requests in good faith and may issue a full or partial refund at our reasonable discretion.

5. Service Issues

If NofoDesk is unavailable for an extended period, or a core feature fails through no fault of your own, contact us at support@foktconsulting.com with details of the issue. We will work to resolve the problem and, where appropriate, may offer a credit, an extension of your subscription, or a refund. Refunds will not be provided where the issue results from misuse, from your own systems or connectivity, or from reliance on extracted information without independent verification.

6. How Refunds Are Issued

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method through our payment processor. Refunds may take several business days to appear, depending on your bank or card issuer. Payment-processing fees may be non-refundable.

7. Disputes

If you believe you have been billed incorrectly, contact us at support@foktconsulting.com within 30 days of the charge. We will review the matter in good faith and may issue a refund or partial refund at our reasonable discretion. We encourage you to contact us before initiating a chargeback so we can resolve the issue directly.

8. Taxes

Subscription fees are stated exclusive of any applicable taxes. Where taxes apply, they are generally non-refundable except as required by law.

9. Contact

Refund and cancellation questions: support@foktconsulting.com
