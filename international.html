Common questions

Something else? Email kelvin@foktconsulting.com or book a 20-minute call.

We received a terms of reference from our board or donor. Can you respond to it?

Yes. Send the ToR and your deadline. You get a proposal within two business days with the approach, the deliverables, the days needed and the price. If a ToR asks for something outside this work, we say so rather than bid.

Do you work on site?

The work is remote by default, which keeps the price down. Where a kickoff, training or a monitoring visit needs to happen in person, travel is agreed and billed at cost under the award's travel rules.

Which rules do you cover?

2 CFR 200 as applied to non-US recipients through the award (including the agency's own standard provisions for non-US organizations), the award's special terms, USAID and CDC reporting formats, and Global Fund grant regulations and reporting. For a question outside those, we tell you where to go.

Can we pay for this from the award?

Consulting fees are generally allowable as professional service costs under 2 CFR 200.459 when they are reasonable and allocable to the award. Check your approved budget and the award terms, and ask your agreement officer if the line is not there.

Are you a CPA firm?

No. We do not audit you, so there is no independence conflict with your auditor or the LFA. Your auditor tests. We build the procedures, the controls and the evidence they test against.

What does the first call cover?

Twenty minutes. Your award or your finding, what the reviewer will check next, your deadline, and whether a Health Check, a fixed-price piece or a consultancy fits. You get a written scope and quote the same day.
