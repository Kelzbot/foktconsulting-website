Common questions

Something else? Email kelvin@foktconsulting.com or book a 20-minute call.

Do you pay referral fees?

No. We don't pay or accept referral fees or commissions. The client pays the published fixed fee and nothing passes between FOKT and your firm.

Who owns the corrective action plan?

The client. Under 2 CFR 200.511 the auditee prepares the corrective action plan. We draft it with owners, dates and evidence. Management reviews it, changes what they want and signs it.

Will you contact us during the work?

Only with the client's written OK. If they agree, we can join a call to walk you through the test map and the corrective actions. We don't represent the client, sign anything for them or argue questioned costs.

Do you need our workpapers?

No. We work from the finding as reported in the Schedule of Findings and Questioned Costs, the client's current plan and their procedures.

Do you need patient or participant records?

No. We don't take patient records, Social Security numbers or other sensitive personal data. We work from de-identified samples or by screen-share, and we sign the client's NDA.

Where do you work?

Remotely, from Abuja, Nigeria, with nonprofits anywhere in the US. Calls run Monday to Friday, 12 to 3 pm Eastern. Emails get a reply within one US business day.
