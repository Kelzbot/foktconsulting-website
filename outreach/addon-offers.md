How to spot the buyer: every FAC finding carries a compliance requirement letter.
A activities allowed · B allowable costs · C cash management · E eligibility · F equipment · G matching / level of effort / earmarking · H period of performance · I procurement, suspension and debarment · J program income · L reporting · M subrecipient monitoring · N special tests.

## 1. Free corrective action plan template (front door)

- **Demand:** "corrective action plan template" 1,900/mo (KD 14), "corrective action plan" 1,600/mo.
- **Who:** Anyone whose audit was accepted in the last 90 days. Their CAP is due now (2 CFR 200.511(c)).
- **What it is:** A fillable CAP built on `samples/corrective-action-plan.html` (already live). Columns: finding number, root cause, action, owner, completion date, and **the record that proves it**. The last column is what most CAPs leave out and what the auditor retests.
- **To build:** A page at `/corrective-action-plan-template` with a .docx and .xlsx download behind an email field, plus a link from the CAP blog post.
- **Next step it sells:** The Finding Check ($650), then the Review.
- **Price:** Free.

## 2. Compliance Supplement pre-audit check

- **Demand:** "compliance supplement 2025" 1,000/mo at KD 9. "omb compliance supplement 2025" 390/mo.
- **Who:** Clients with a fiscal year ending in the next 3 months. Also anyone who just finished a Review and wants the next audit clean.
- **Deliverable:** For each likely major program, the Supplement's Part 4 matrix (which of the 12 requirements apply), what the auditor will sample for each one, and where their records fall short. Two to three pages.
- **Scope edge:** You can't promise which programs the auditor picks as major. The auditor decides (2 CFR 200.518). Say "likely major programs".
- **Leads to:** The Audit Readiness Retainer.
- **Suggested price:** $1,500, or the first month of the retainer.
- **Check before selling:** Is the 2026 Supplement out, and does the 2025 edition still apply to this client's fiscal year?

## 3. Indirect cost and cost allocation setup (new)

- **Demand:** "indirect cost rate" 210, "what are indirect costs in a grant" 210 (KD 5), "de minimis rate" 170, "cost allocation plan" 140 ($9.86 CPC).
- **Who:** B findings that mention indirect costs, allocation, shared costs or the de minimis rate. Also orgs with several awards and no negotiated rate (NICRA).
- **Deliverables:**
  1. Rate decision memo: the de minimis 15% of MTDC vs applying for a negotiated rate (2 CFR 200.414(f)). Include what each one recovers on their real budget.
  2. Written cost allocation method for shared costs such as rent, IT, finance staff and program managers on several awards (200.405).
  3. A direct vs indirect classification list that is applied the same way every time (200.412, 200.413, consistency in 200.403).
  4. Test 10 posted charges against the method and list the fixes.
- **Scope edge:** If they want a NICRA, you prepare the indirect cost proposal and they submit it to their cognizant agency. You don't negotiate the rate for them.
- **NofoDesk tie-in:** Its budget engine already applies MTDC exclusions and the de minimis rate.
- **Suggested price:** $2,500 (method and memo), $4,000 with proposal preparation.

## 4. Time-and-effort fix (new)

- **Demand:** "time and effort" 260/mo at KD 8. "time and effort reporting" 110/mo ($5.93 CPC).
- **Who:** B findings about payroll, personnel charges, timesheets, or "budget estimates not reconciled".
- **Deliverables:** A personnel-cost documentation procedure that meets 2 CFR 200.430(i): records reflect the person's total activity, sit inside the internal control system, and are reconciled when budget estimates were used. Also a timesheet or certification template, an after-the-fact reconciliation worksheet, and a 30-minute walkthrough for the payroll team.
- **NofoDesk tie-in:** The Level of Effort comparison already flags allocation changes month to month. Certification sign-off is on the build list.
- **Suggested price:** $1,500.

## 5. Staff and board compliance training (new)

- **Demand:** Small in search ("grant compliance training" 50/mo, $5.95 CPC). Sell it in conversation and through state nonprofit associations.
- **Who:** Every Review client. Training is often one of the CAP's own action steps, so the attendance record becomes evidence.
- **Deliverable:** A 90-minute live session on their own findings, with slides, an attendance sheet and a short quiz. Keep the sign-in and quiz results as CAP evidence.
- **Already inside:** CAP Implementation Support ($4,500) includes training. This is the standalone version.
- **Suggested price:** $1,200 per session.

## 6. Subrecipient monitoring program (new)

- **Demand:** "subrecipient monitoring" 90/mo. Low volume, but every buyer is a pass-through entity with money going out.
- **Who:** M findings, and any org that gives subawards. A retest note already exists: `outreach/retest-notes/subrecipient-monitoring.md`.
- **Deliverables:** A subrecipient vs contractor determination checklist (200.331), a risk assessment form and scoring, a checklist of what each subaward agreement must contain, a monitoring plan by risk level, a routine to check each sub's Single Audit on the FAC, and a log for following up on the sub's findings (2 CFR 200.332, 200.521).
- **NofoDesk tie-in:** Agreements and signature tracking are built. Monitoring is on the build list.
- **Suggested price:** $3,000.

## 7. Fractional grant accountant (new, decide first)

- **Demand:** "grant accounting" 480/mo, "fractional cfo for nonprofits" 140/mo ($22.87 CPC), "bookkeeping for non profit" 210/mo ($16.40).
- **What it is:** Monthly close review, budget vs actual for each award, SF-425 preparation, and drawdown reconciliation (200.305).
- **Catch:** This is ongoing operational work in their accounting system, mostly QuickBooks. It is not findings work. Take it only if you want recurring hours. You're not a CPA firm, so offer no audit or attest work.
- **Suggested price:** $1,500 to $2,500 a month, depending on the number of awards.

## 8. NofoDesk for US clients

- **Now:** Sell it as the workspace for award setup and deadlines. Don't pitch it to US post-award buyers as a full system until budget vs actual and the findings tracker exist (`outreach/nofodesk-gap-plan.md`).
- **Later:** Include a NofoDesk seat in every Findings Review and retainer. The client tracks their CAP in it, and you see their evidence.

| Messy books behind every finding | 7. Fractional (if you're offering it) |
