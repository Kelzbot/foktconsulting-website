# Outreach kit (not published)

This folder is listed in `.assetsignore`, so nothing here reaches the live site.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `email-sequence-us-nonprofits.md` | The 3-email cold sequence for US nonprofits with a Single Audit finding. Send in batches of 30 to 50, open tracking off, 3 to 4 days apart. |
| `email-sequence-international.md` | The 3-email sequence for international NGOs and implementing partners with US or Global Fund awards. |
| `eoi-template.md` | Expression of interest and short proposal, for consultancy calls that come with a terms of reference. |
| `boards.md` | Where consultancy calls are posted, what to search for, and how the daily scan reports them. |
| `call-script.md` | The 20-minute call, in order, and the same-day proposal that follows. |

Rules that apply to everything here:

- Kelvin sends every email and submits every bid himself. Agents draft, Kelvin reads, edits and sends.
- Never name the employer. "A Nigerian health research institute funded by PEPFAR, NIH, USAID, the Global Fund and WHO" is the agreed wording, the same as the website.
- Check the employment contract (outside work, conflict of interest, confidentiality) before approaching any organization that is a peer or partner of the employer.
- Two personal details in every first email: something specific from their audit report or their award, and something specific about them or their organization that is not on the finding.
- No invented numbers, results or clients. Until there are client results, the proof is the portfolio, the deliverables and the samples on the site.
