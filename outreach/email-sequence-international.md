# 3-email sequence: international NGOs and implementing partners

Who: the finance director, grants manager, compliance lead or country director of a non-US organization that holds, or has just won, a USAID, CDC, NIH, PEPFAR or Global Fund award, or is a sub-recipient of a US prime.

Where the names come from: USAID and CDC award announcements, the Global Fund grant portfolio by country, prime recipients' partner lists, job posts for "grants compliance" roles (an organization hiring for it has the problem now), and consultancy calls on the boards in `boards.md`.

Same rules as the US sequence: batches of 30 to 50, open tracking off, plain text, two personal details, stop on reply. Do not approach peers or partners of the employer until the employment contract has been checked.
