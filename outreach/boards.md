# Consultancy boards and the daily scan

## Where calls are posted

| Board | URL | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| ReliefWeb jobs | https://reliefweb.int/jobs | Filter: Consultancy. The biggest source of NGO ToRs. |
| DevelopmentAid | https://www.developmentaid.org/ | Tenders and "experts" calls. Some need a paid account to see the full ToR. |
| Devex | https://www.devex.com/jobs | Consultant roles and funding opportunities. Partly paywalled. |
| Coordination SUD | https://www.coordinationsud.org/ | French NGO network. Posts in French. Compliance calls for US and UN funding appear regularly. |
| Idealist | https://www.idealist.org/ | US nonprofits and international NGOs. Filter: consultant or contract. |
| UNGM | https://www.ungm.org/ | UN procurement notices, including compliance and finance consultancies. Free account needed to download. |
| NGO job boards by country | e.g. NGO Jobs Nigeria, Kenya, Uganda, Ethiopia | Country offices post locally first. |
| Organizations' own sites | The Freedom Fund, Islamic Relief, CARE, IRC, FHI 360, Pact, Jhpiego, PSI, Chemonics, DAI, RTI | Check "work with us" or "procurement" pages. |

## Search terms

Use these on every board, in English and the French equivalents on Coordination SUD:

- "2 CFR 200", "Uniform Guidance", "USG compliance", "US government compliance"
- "USAID compliance", "CDC compliance", "PEPFAR compliance"
- "subaward", "sub-recipient monitoring", "subgrant"
- "Single Audit", "audit findings", "corrective action plan"
- "grants compliance consultant", "donor compliance consultant", "financial compliance review"
- "Global Fund" with "compliance", "LFA", "audit"
- French: "conformité bailleur", "conformité USAID", "financement américain", "audit de conformité", "sous-subvention"

## What a match looks like

Score each call 0 to 3 and only bring forward 2s and 3s:

- **3:** The ToR names 2 CFR 200, USAID/CDC/PEPFAR rules or Global Fund grant regulations, the work is remote or in West Africa, and the deadline is at least 4 days away.
- **2:** Donor compliance or subaward work for an NGO on US or Global Fund money, remote possible, deadline at least 4 days away.
- **1:** General finance, M&E or audit work where compliance is a part. Log it, do not bid.
- **0:** On-site only outside the region, requires a CPA or audit licence, or deadline passed.

## Daily scan output

A short digest, each morning, in this format:

```
FOKT consultancy scan, [date]

NEW (score 3)
- [Organization]: [ToR title]. Deadline [date]. Remote: [yes/no/unclear]. Link.
  Why it fits: [one line quoting the ToR].
  Draft EOI: [ready / needs these answers from Kelvin: ...]

NEW (score 2)
- ...

STILL OPEN from earlier scans (deadline within 7 days)
- ...

Nothing matched on: [boards checked with no result]
```

Kelvin decides what to bid on. The scan never submits anything.
