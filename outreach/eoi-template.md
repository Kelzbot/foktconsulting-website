# Expression of interest and short proposal

For consultancy calls that come with a terms of reference (ToR). Reply within two business days of finding the call, and always before the deadline minus two days: a late or rushed bid loses more than a missed one.

Read the ToR twice. Answer in the order the ToR asks. If it asks for a CV, a rate and references, send exactly those, in that order, as separate PDFs with clear file names (`FOKT_Fawehinmi_CV.pdf`, `FOKT_Proposal_[Org]_[Month].pdf`).

Delete every bracket before sending.

## Proposal (2 to 4 pages)

### 1. Understanding of the assignment

Two short paragraphs. First: what the organization is trying to achieve, in their words, and why now (new award, audit finding, donor review, growth in subawards). Second: what makes it hard for a non-US organization, from experience, e.g. US procurement thresholds that do not match local markets, timekeeping rules that assume salaried US staff, subaward monitoring across partners with different systems.

### 2. Approach and methodology

One numbered phase per ToR deliverable, each with: what is done, who from their side is needed and for how long, and what they receive. Keep the deliverable names identical to the ToR's. Example skeleton:

1. **Inception (week 1).** Document review and three interviews. Output: inception note with the work plan and the gap register format agreed.
2. **Gap analysis (weeks 2 to 3).** Award terms, 2 CFR 200 as applied, and current practice compared item by item. Output: gap register and test map.
3. **Policies and procedures (weeks 4 to 7).** Drafted, reviewed with the owners, revised once. Output: procedures manual and templates.
4. **Training and handover (week 8).** Two sessions, recorded. Output: slides, recordings, and a 25-item self-test the organization runs itself.

### 3. Why FOKT

Four points, no more:

- Day-to-day experience running the same awards inside an implementing partner, under the same rules the ToR cites.
- Deliverables the reviewer can test: every procedure names the record it leaves. Samples at foktconsulting.com/samples/test-map.
- Remote and in the region's time zones, which keeps the price down and the response fast.
- Not an audit firm, so no independence conflict with the organization's auditor or the donor's reviewers.

### 4. Work plan and level of effort

A table: phase, weeks, days of consultant time, days of the organization's time. Total days at the bottom.

### 5. Price

Day rate × total days = total, in US dollars, stated as excluding any withholding tax the organization is required to apply. Payment: `[e.g. 30% on inception note, 40% on gap register and draft procedures, 30% on final handover]`. Validity: 30 days.

If the ToR asks for a daily rate only, give the rate and the number of days, and nothing else.

### 6. Consultant

Half a page: the portfolio managed (donors, scale, subawards), the lifecycle covered (pre-award to close-out), education, languages, location. Link to LinkedIn. No employer name.

### 7. References

Only if asked, and only people who have agreed in advance.

## Before sending

- Every deliverable in the ToR appears in section 2 under the same name.
- The price is in the currency and format the ToR asked for.
- The file names, the page limit and the submission address match the ToR.
- Nothing names the employer or a client that does not exist.
