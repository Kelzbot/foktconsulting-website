# Reviewer checklists (drafts)

The one-pagers offered in `email-sequence-international.md`. One per donor. Each is a draft written from the rules as Kelvin knows them. **Before any of these goes to a prospect, Kelvin checks every citation against the current text of the rule and the award's own standard provisions, and removes anything he is not certain of.** Items marked `[verify]` are the ones most likely to have changed.

Send as a one-page PDF on FOKT letterhead, with the footer: "FOKT Consulting LLC · foktconsulting.com/international · This note is general information, not advice on your award. Your agreement and its standard provisions control."
