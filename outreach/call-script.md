# The 20-minute call, and the proposal that follows

Have open: their audit report or award letter, the site's pricing section, a blank proposal.

## Minute 0 to 2: frame it

"Thanks for the time. In twenty minutes I want to understand the finding `[or the award]`, tell you what the auditor `[or reviewer]` will check next, and then say whether and how I can help. If I cannot, I will say so. Sound right?"

## Minute 2 to 7: their finding or award

Ask, then listen:

- "Walk me through `[finding number]` as you understand it. What did the auditor say the cause was?"
- "What has changed since the report?" (Usually: the policy. Rarely: the step or the record.)
- "Who owns this inside the organization?"
- For an award: "Which parts of the agreement worry you most?" and "When is the first report or review?"

## Minute 7 to 11: what the auditor or reviewer checks next

Tell them, specifically, from the Compliance Supplement or the award terms. Two or three items, with the record each one needs. This is the proof that you know the work. Do not sell yet.

## Minute 11 to 13: the deadline

"When is the next audit fieldwork?" or "When is the review or the report due?" Write the date down. Everything in the proposal counts back from it.

## Minute 13 to 16: the fix, in three steps

Diagnose (test map), fix (procedures and the corrective action plan), prove (self-test and the status note). Say what their team does and how many hours it takes. Say what they receive.

## Minute 16 to 19: the offer

Pick one, out loud, and say why:

- **Health Check, $650, one week.** When they are unsure, when the finding area is unclear, or when the budget needs a small first yes.
- **Findings Review, $2,000, two weeks.** When the finding is clear and the audit is within four months.
- **Consultancy from their ToR.** When they already have a ToR or need more than one area fixed.

Then: "I will send a one-page proposal today with the scope, the dates and the price. If it looks right, reply yes and I send the invoice. Work starts when the deposit arrives."

## Minute 19 to 20: close

"Anything I have not asked that I should know?" Then thank them and end on time.

## Same day: the one-page proposal

Email, plain text or a one-page PDF:

1. Their finding or award, in one sentence, and the date it counts back from.
2. What they receive, as a list, with the dates.
3. Their time: hours and who.
4. Price and payment terms.
5. One line: "Reply yes to start, and I will send the invoice."

Follow up once, three business days later, if there is no reply.
