**Why this finding keeps coming back.** The purchasing policy gets rewritten, but the people placing orders keep buying the way they always did, and nothing in the purchase file proves that quotes were compared or that the vendor was checked. The auditor samples purchases made after the corrective action plan and looks in the file. If the comparison or the exclusion check is not there, the finding repeats, whatever the policy says.

| What the auditor will pull and test | Evidence that closes it |
|---|---|
| **A sample of purchases above the micro-purchase threshold**, each traced to its purchase file. Often 25 to 60 items, set by risk and population. | One purchase file per item, built from a checklist, with every document below in it. |
| **That the method matches the amount**: micro-purchase, informal quotes, or formal competition. (2 CFR 200.320) | The method written on the file. A threshold table in the policy, copied from your own current policy, not a template. |
| **Written procurement procedures** that reflect the federal standards. (2 CFR 200.318(a)) | A dated, board-approved policy. Training sign-in for everyone who places or approves orders. |
| **Competition**: price or rate quotes from an adequate number of sources, and the reason the winner was chosen. | A one-page quote comparison, signed by the approver **before** the order. |
| **Cost or price analysis** above the simplified acquisition threshold. (2 CFR 200.324) | A short memo filed before the award, stating the method used. |
| **Suspension and debarment check** before a covered transaction. (2 CFR 180.300, 200.214) | A dated SAM.gov exclusion search, printed or saved to the file before the order. Not once a year for the biggest vendors. |
| **Conflict of interest** standards for the people who buy. (2 CFR 200.318(c)(1)) | An annual signed disclosure from everyone who approves purchases, kept in HR. |
| **Required contract clauses** on contracts above the simplified acquisition threshold. (2 CFR 200.327, Appendix II) | A clause rider attached to each contract. |
| **Sole-source purchases** and their justification. (2 CFR 200.320(c)) | A written justification on the file, approved before the order. |
| **Procurement records**: method, selection, basis for the price. (2 CFR 200.318(i)) | The file checklist, completed for every purchase above the micro-purchase threshold. |
| **A self-test.** | 25 purchases made after the fix date, checked against the checklist, zero exceptions, filed for the auditor. |

**A 30-day fix plan**

| Week 1 | Week 2 | Week 3 | Week 4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pull the sampled purchases from the audit and your corrective action plan. For each exception, name the document that was missing. | Rewrite the policy with your current thresholds. Build the purchase file checklist and the quote comparison sheet. | Start using the checklist on every new purchase. Run the SAM.gov check before every order. Collect conflict of interest disclosures. | Self-test 25 new purchases. File the evidence. Send your funder a CAP status note. |

**A corrective action plan entry auditors accept:** "Effective [date], every purchase above the micro-purchase threshold is placed only after the Procurement Officer completes the purchase file checklist, including a signed quote comparison and a dated SAM.gov exclusion check, and the Finance Director reviews a sample of 10 files each quarter. Evidence: completed checklists, the quarterly review log, and a 25-item self-test." Every entry names a person, a date and the evidence you will show.
