**Why this finding keeps coming back.** Most organizations that pass federal money to partners have a subaward template. What they often lack is a record that anyone assessed the partner's risk before signing, or looked at what the partner did afterwards. The auditor samples your subrecipients and asks for three things: the signed agreement with the required information, the risk assessment, and proof of monitoring. A gap in any one repeats the finding.

| What the auditor will pull and test | Evidence that closes it |
|---|---|
| **A written decision** that each partner is a subrecipient or a contractor. (2 CFR 200.331) | A one-page determination in each partner file, signed and dated, using the criteria in the rule. |
| **That each subaward carries the required information**: the federal award identification, the applicable terms, the indirect cost rate, and the audit requirement. (2 CFR 200.332) | A subaward template that has every required field, and a checklist completed when each subaward is signed. |
| **A risk evaluation before the award**, covering things like prior audit results, experience with similar awards and the quality of their systems. (2 CFR 200.332) | A risk assessment form completed and scored before signing, with the monitoring level it leads to. |
| **Monitoring that fits the risk**: reports reviewed, desk reviews, site visits, depending on risk. (2 CFR 200.332) | A monitoring plan per subrecipient (what, how often, who). The review of each financial and program report on file. Site visit notes. |
| **That the partner's audit was obtained and its findings followed up**, when they are required to have one. (2 CFR 200.332, 200.501) | A tracking sheet: partner, audit due date, date received, findings, your action, date closed. |
| **That the partner is not suspended or debarred.** (2 CFR 180.300) | A dated SAM.gov check before the award, and again on renewal. |
| **Required subaward reporting** to the awarding agency. (2 CFR 170) [verify the current requirement for your award] | The report submission confirmation on file for each subaward that triggers it. |
| **Management decision on findings** within the required time after you receive the report. (2 CFR 200.521) | Dated written decision letters. |
| **A self-test.** | For 10 subrecipients (or all, if fewer), check each for the five documents above, with zero gaps, filed for the auditor. |

**A 30-day fix plan**

| Week 1 | Week 2 | Week 3 | Week 4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| List every partner paid with this award. Mark each as subrecipient or contractor and find the file for each. | Update the subaward template and build the risk assessment form and the monitoring plan format. | Complete a risk assessment and monitoring plan for every current subrecipient. Run the SAM.gov check. Chase missing partner audits. | Self-test the files. Close the gaps. Send your funder a CAP status note. |

**A corrective action plan entry auditors accept:** "Effective [date], the Grants Manager completes a risk assessment and a written monitoring plan for each subrecipient before the subaward is signed, and reviews each subrecipient's financial report within 30 days of receipt. The Finance Director checks the tracking sheet quarterly. Evidence: signed risk assessments, monitoring plans, report review logs and the audit tracking sheet." Every entry names a person, a date and the evidence you will show.
