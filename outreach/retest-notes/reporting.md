**Why this finding keeps coming back.** A report goes out late, or a figure on it does not match the ledger, and the fix is to correct that one report. Next year's reports are built the same way: from a spreadsheet nobody reviewed, with no record of where each number came from. The auditor picks new-year reports and traces each line back to the books. If the trail is missing, the finding repeats.

| What the auditor will pull and test | Evidence that closes it |
|---|---|
| **A sample of financial and program reports**, each submitted on time per the award terms. (2 CFR 200.328, 200.329) | A reporting calendar listing every report, due date, owner and reviewer, kept for the whole award period. The submission confirmation filed with each report. |
| **That every key line ties to the general ledger**: expenditures, cash received, program income, unobligated balance. | A reconciliation workpaper per report, tying each line to the ledger report it came from, dated and initialed. |
| **That the report was reviewed before submission** by someone other than the preparer. | A reviewer's sign-off on the workpaper, with the date. |
| **That the numbers are consistent across reports**: the report, the drawdowns and the Schedule of Expenditures of Federal Awards agree. (2 CFR 200.510) | A quarterly cross-check of reports, draws and the SEFA workpaper. |
| **Performance or program reports** that match the data in program records. (2 CFR 200.329) | Source data for each reported figure, and who verified it. |
| **Required subaward reporting** to the awarding agency. (2 CFR 170) [verify the current requirement for your award] | The submission confirmation for each subaward that triggers it. |
| **Timely audit submission** to the FAC. (2 CFR 200.512) [verify the current deadline wording] | The audit report date, the FAC acceptance date, and the calculation showing you were on time. |
| **A self-test.** | Every report filed after the fix date, checked against its workpaper, zero exceptions, filed for the auditor. |

**A 30-day fix plan**

| Week 1 | Week 2 | Week 3 | Week 4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pull the reports the auditor tested and your corrective action plan. For each, find which line did not tie, or how late it was. | Build the reporting calendar from the award terms. Write the workpaper template and the reviewer sign-off step. | Prepare the next report on the new template, reviewed by a second person before submission. | Self-test all reports filed since the fix. File the evidence. Send your funder a CAP status note. |

**A corrective action plan entry auditors accept:** "Effective [date], the Grants Accountant prepares each federal financial report from the general ledger using the reconciliation workpaper, and the Finance Director reviews and initials the workpaper before submission. A reporting calendar tracks every due date, and the Executive Director receives it monthly. Evidence: calendar, signed workpapers, submission confirmations and a self-test of all reports filed since the fix." Every entry names a person, a date and the evidence you will show.
