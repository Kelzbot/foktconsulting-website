**Why this finding keeps coming back.** Intake staff know the rules and serve people who qualify, but the file doesn't prove it. A pay stub is missing, the residency document is dated after services started, or the person who signed the determination wasn't authorized to. The program's eligibility rules live in the award and in Part 4 of the Supplement, not in 2 CFR 200, so a general policy rewrite doesn't fix it. The auditor picks participant files and checks each criterion against the document in the file, as of the date of service. If one file in the sample can't prove one criterion, the finding repeats, and the cost of serving that person can be questioned.

| What the auditor will pull and test | Evidence that closes it |
|---|---|
| **A sample of participant or beneficiary files** from the program tested. (Supplement Part 3.E) | A file for every person served, with a checklist listing each eligibility criterion from the award and Part 4, and the document that proves it. |
| **That every criterion was met when services started**, not documented afterwards. (Part 4, program section) | Documents dated on or before the first date of service, and a determination date recorded on the checklist. |
| **Who made the determination**, and that they were authorized to. (2 CFR 200.303) | A signed determination by a named, trained staff member, with a second review for a sample or for every file. A written list of who may approve. |
| **That the benefit or service amount was calculated correctly**, where the program sets amounts by income, household size or need. (Part 3.E) | The calculation sheet in the file, using the program's current table, checked by a second person. |
| **Re-determinations on the program's schedule**, where eligibility must be renewed. (Part 4, program section) [verify the schedule for your program] | A tracker of each participant's next re-determination date, and the renewal documents in the file. |
| **That costs for ineligible participants were not charged to the award.** (2 CFR 200.403) | An exceptions log: each person found ineligible, the costs involved, the date they were moved off the award, and the adjustment entry. |
| **Subrecipients that determine eligibility** for you, if any. (Part 3.E) | The eligibility requirements written into each subaward, and your monitoring of a sample of their files. |
| **Records kept and retrievable.** (2 CFR 200.334) | Files stored so any one can be pulled by participant name or ID within minutes, for the full retention period. |
| **A self-test.** | Every file opened after the fix date, checked against the checklist, zero exceptions, filed for the auditor. |

**A corrective action plan entry auditors accept:** "Effective [date], the Intake Coordinator completes the eligibility checklist for each applicant before services begin, attaching the document that proves each criterion. The Program Manager reviews and signs every determination before the first service date. The Grants Accountant reviews the exceptions log monthly and moves any costs for ineligible participants off the award. Evidence: completed checklists, signed determinations, the exceptions log and a self-test of every file opened since the fix." Every entry names a person, a date and the evidence you will show.
