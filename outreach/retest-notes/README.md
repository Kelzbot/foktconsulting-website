## Tailoring a note in about 10 minutes

1. Open the prospect's audit report from the FAC (app.fac.gov, their audit, "audit report PDF"). Find the finding in the Schedule of Findings and Questioned Costs.
2. Fill the grey **YOUR FINDING** block at the top of the note: organization, finding number, program and ALN, the auditor's firm name, one sentence of the condition in the auditor's own words, and the cause if stated. Say whether it is a repeat and the prior finding number.
3. In the table, **bold or move up** the one or two rows that match the condition. Delete a row only if the finding clearly does not touch it.
4. Rewrite the CAP example so it names the roles in their organization (use the titles from their audit report or website). Keep the structure: person, date, evidence.
5. Export to a one-page PDF (open the markdown in a browser or a Word document, print to PDF, A4 or Letter). Name it `FOKT_Retest_Note_[Org]_[FindingNumber].pdf`.
6. Send it within one US business day of the "yes", from kelvin@foktconsulting.com, with a two-line email: the note is attached, and the Finding Check ($650, credited against a Findings Review) is the next step if they want it fixed.

## Rules for every note

- Facts come from the rule or the audit report. Never invent a result, a client or a number.
- Citations are to 2 CFR 200 as revised effective October 1, 2024 and the OMB Compliance Supplement. Items tagged `[verify]` are the ones most likely to have changed or be misnumbered. Check each against the current eCFR before the first send, and remove the tag once checked.
- No dollar thresholds in the notes. They change with FAR inflation adjustments, and the table says "the micro-purchase threshold" or "the simplified acquisition threshold" and tells the reader to use the figure in their own policy.
- The footer on every note: "General guidance based on public Federal Audit Clearinghouse data and 2 CFR 200 as of October 2026. Not legal or audit advice. FOKT is not a CPA firm and does not perform audits."
