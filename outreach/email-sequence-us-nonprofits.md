# 3-email sequence: US nonprofits with a Single Audit finding

Who: the finance director, controller or executive director of a US nonprofit whose latest Single Audit (on the Federal Audit Clearinghouse) shows a finding, especially a repeat finding.

Batch of 30 to 50 at a time. Open tracking off. Plain text, no images, no attachments on email 1. Send Tuesday to Thursday, 9 to 11 am in their time zone. Gaps: email 2 three to four days after email 1, email 3 four to five days after email 2. Stop the sequence the moment they reply.

Fill every `[bracket]` from the audit report and from their website or LinkedIn. If you cannot fill both personal details, do not send.

---

## After a reply

- "Send the note": send it within one US business day, matched to their finding. End the note with one line: "If you want this fixed before the next audit, book 20 minutes here: cal.com/foktconsulting/20min".
- "Not now": thank them, ask whether the next audit is before or after a date, and set a reminder for two months before it.
- A call booked: use `call-script.md`.
