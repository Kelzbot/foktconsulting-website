4. Where the money is: add-ons to the Findings Review

Each offer below is tied to search demand from this data and to FOKT's existing ladder: Finding Check $650, Findings Review $2,000, UG policies set $2,500, CAP Implementation Support $4,500, Audit Readiness Retainer $1,000/mo. Gold border = new offer. No prices are set for the new ones. That's your call.

1. Free CAP template as the front door

CAP template (free, email) → Finding Check $650 → Findings Review $2,000 → CAP Implementation $4,500

Demand: "corrective action plan template" 1,900/mo (KD 14), "corrective action plan" 1,600/mo. Thompson's template page alone gets about 186 visits a month.

Why first: No new service to build, and the buyer who downloads it already has a finding. You already have the matching post.

2. Compliance Supplement pre-audit check

Year-named Supplement page → pre-audit check → Audit Readiness Retainer $1,000/mo

Demand: "compliance supplement 2025" 1,000/mo at KD 9, the easiest high-volume term in the set. Add "omb compliance supplement 2025" at 390/mo.

Timing: Publish when OMB issues the 2026 Supplement. Its status is already on the re-check list.

3. Indirect cost and cost allocation setup

Indirect-cost post → setup engagement → UG policies set $2,500

Demand: "indirect cost rate" 210, "what are indirect costs in a grant" 210 (KD 5), "de minimis rate" 170, "cost allocation plan" 140 ($9.86 CPC). Total about 730/mo, all low difficulty.

Scope: Choose de minimis or a negotiated rate (2 CFR 200.414), write the allocation method, and test that charges are allocable (200.405). It prevents allowable-cost findings.

4. Time-and-effort fix

Time-and-effort post → payroll documentation setup → retainer

Demand: "time and effort" 260/mo at KD 8. "time and effort reporting" 110/mo at $5.93 CPC.

Scope: Build personnel-cost records that meet 2 CFR 200.430(i). Payroll is usually the largest cost on an award, and auditors test it under Allowable Costs.

5. Staff and board compliance training

Standalone workshop → CAP Implementation (already includes training)

Demand: Small in search: "grant compliance training" 50/mo ($5.95 CPC), "federal grant management training" 50/mo. Thompson built a business on this. Sell it through state associations (memberclicks link above) and outreach, not SEO.

6. Subrecipient monitoring program

Existing subrecipient post → risk assessment + monitoring plan → retainer

Demand: "subrecipient monitoring" only 90/mo, but every buyer is a pass-through entity with subawards, required under 2 CFR 200.332. Low volume, high fit. Push it through FAC outreach to orgs with subrecipient findings.

7. Fractional grant accountant

Monthly close review + grant reporting → replaces or sits beside the retainer

Demand: "grant accounting" 480/mo, "fractional cfo for nonprofits" 140/mo ($22.87 CPC), "bookkeeping for non profit" 210 ($16.40), "nonprofit budget" 1,600. This is how JMCO and Hungerford earn recurring revenue.

Catch: It's ongoing operational work, not findings work. Decide whether you want it before writing content for it.

8. NofoDesk on a software search

Software landing page → NofoDesk trial

Demand: "grant management software for nonprofits" 590/mo at $44.88 CPC, the highest commercial value in this data.

Fit: NofoDesk already covers the pre-award side and award setup (NOFO and Notice of Award reading, conditions clock, reporting calendar, budgets with indirect rules, team workspace). US post-award buyers will also ask for budget vs actual, SF-425 and drawdown tracking, and subrecipient monitoring, which it doesn't have yet. See outreach/nofodesk-gap-plan.md.

The long game: a 2 CFR 200 hub

SmartGrant gets about half its traffic from one 2 CFR 200 guide. The top four variants ("2 cfr 200", "2 cfr part 200", "2cfr200", "uniform guidance") total 7,500 searches a month, but KD is 26–45 and FOKT's Authority Score is 2. Start with single-section pages that are easy to rank ("2 cfr 200 procurement" KD 19, "2 cfr 200.475" KD 17), link them to a hub, and point the hub at the $2,500 UG policies set.

Suggested order

  1. Fix the link base first: Crunchbase, Pitchbook, Curlie, Substack. They're free, you can do them this week, and nothing ranks at AS 2.
  2. CAP template page plus email capture (offer 1).
  3. Indirect cost and time-and-effort posts. Both are KD under 10 and lead into offers 3 and 4.
  4. 2026 Compliance Supplement page the week OMB releases it (offer 2).
  5. Pitch university research offices and your state nonprofit association once the template is live.
