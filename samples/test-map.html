Test map: Procurement, suspension and debarment (I)

Requirement I of the OMB Compliance Supplement, Part 3, against the organization's current practice. Prepared after the kickoff call and the document review. One row per thing the auditor tests.

RefWhat the auditor testsSourceWhat you do nowGapEvidence the fix must leave
I-1 Written procurement procedures exist and reflect the federal standards. 2 CFR 200.318(a) A 2019 purchasing policy. It does not mention the micro-purchase or simplified acquisition thresholds or conflict of interest. Partial Revised policy, board approval minute, date of adoption.
I-2 Purchases above the micro-purchase threshold have price or rate quotations from an adequate number of sources. 2 CFR 200.320(a)(2) Staff collect quotes by email. Nothing records how many were asked or why the winner was chosen. Open One-page quote comparison in every purchase file above the threshold, signed by the approver.
I-3 Cost or price analysis is performed for procurements above the simplified acquisition threshold. 2 CFR 200.324(a) None performed. Two purchases in the audit period were above the threshold. Open Cost or price analysis memo filed before the award, with the method stated.
I-4 The organization checks that vendors are not suspended or debarred before a covered transaction. 2 CFR 180.300, 200.214 Checked once a year for the largest vendors. Not checked per transaction, and no record kept. Open SAM.gov exclusion search printout, dated before the order, in the purchase file.
I-5 Written standards of conduct cover conflicts of interest in procurement. 2 CFR 200.318(c)(1) Conflict of interest policy exists for the board. Staff who make purchases sign nothing. Partial Annual signed disclosure for everyone who approves purchases, kept with HR records.
I-6 Procurement records are kept: rationale for the method, contractor selection and the basis for the price. 2 CFR 200.318(i) Invoices and purchase orders kept. Selection rationale kept for none of the sampled items. Open Purchase file checklist, completed for every purchase above the micro-purchase threshold.
I-7 Contracts above the simplified acquisition threshold contain the required provisions. 2 CFR 200.327, Appendix II Contracts use the vendor's own terms. The two contracts above the threshold lack Appendix II clauses. Open Appendix II clause rider attached to each contract above the threshold.
I-8 Micro-purchases are distributed equitably among qualified suppliers where practicable. 2 CFR 200.320(a)(1)(ii) Small purchases rotate between local suppliers in practice. Met No change. Note the practice in the revised policy.
