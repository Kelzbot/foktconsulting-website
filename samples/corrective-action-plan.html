Corrective action plan

Prepared for the auditee's management in the form required by 2 CFR 200.511(c): the contact person, the action planned for each finding, and the anticipated completion date. Each action names the evidence that shows it works, so the next audit can test it.

Finding
2025-002, Procurement, suspension and debarment (I). Repeat of 2024-004.
Program
Community Services Block Grant, ALN 93.569, pass-through from the state agency
Condition (summary)
For 6 of 25 purchases sampled above the micro-purchase threshold, no quote comparison, price analysis or exclusion check was on file.
Contact person
Director of Finance (name, email and phone on the signed version)
Plan date
The date management signs the plan
ActionWhat changesOwnerCompletion dateEvidence that it works
1 Adopt the revised procurement policy with the current thresholds, the conflict of interest standard and the purchase file checklist. Train everyone who approves purchases. Director of Finance Month 1, day 30 Board minute approving the policy. Signed training attendance sheet.
2 Every purchase above the micro-purchase threshold gets a one-page quote comparison, signed by the approver, before the order is placed. Procurement Officer From month 2, day 1 Comparison sheet in the purchase file. Finance checks a sample of 10 each quarter.
3 An exclusion check on SAM.gov is run and the dated result filed before any covered transaction. Procurement Officer From month 2, day 1 Dated SAM.gov search result in the purchase file.
4 A cost or price analysis memo is written before any award above the simplified acquisition threshold, and Appendix II clauses are attached to the contract. Director of Finance From month 2, day 1 Analysis memo and clause rider in the contract file.
5 Self-test: 25 purchases dated after month 2, checked against the purchase file checklist. Exceptions are corrected and the cause recorded. Finance Manager, with FOKT Month 3, day 30 Completed self-test workbook with the sampled items and any exceptions.
6 Status note sent to the pass-through entity with the plan, the self-test result and the evidence list. Executive Director Month 4, day 15 Copy of the note and the pass-through entity's acknowledgement.

Illustrative sample. The organization, the finding and the dates are invented to show the format. In a Findings Review the plan is written on your letterhead in your own words, with real names and dates, and the actions come directly from the open rows of the test map. See the sample test map.
